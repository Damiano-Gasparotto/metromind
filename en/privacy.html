Privacy & cookies

What data MetroMind uses, where it stays and how to manage it.

Text updated on 11 October 2026 · by Damiano Gasparotto

Project operator

MetroMind is operated by Damiano Gasparotto. Contact the author through the GitHub profile. General issues can be reported in the project repository; issues are public and are not suitable for sensitive information.

Game data

The game does not require an account, name or email address. Missions, routes, times and visited stops are processed in the browser and remain in page memory. The game code does not send results to a server and does not include audience analytics.

Your language preference is stored locally under metromind-language. It remains until you clear the site’s data or use another browser. A service worker caches game files for offline play. You can remove both forms of storage through your browser’s site-data settings for metromind.it.

Hosting and network requests

The website is hosted with Sites. To deliver pages, the hosting service receives the normal information in a web request, such as IP address, requested resource and browser information. Technical handling of these requests depends on the hosting provider. We do not use this information to create a player profile.

Advertising and cookies

MetroMind is prepared for possible Google AdSense advertising. In this version the ad area is hidden and the game does not load the ad-serving code. The verification meta tag and ads.txt identify the publisher; they do not serve ads by themselves.

If advertising is enabled, Google and other providers may use cookies to show ads based on visits to this and other websites. Consent choices will be presented through Google’s consent platform, and this page will be updated to reflect the actual configuration. Read Google’s advertising information and manage personalisation through My Ad Center.

External links

Operator sources and GitHub open on their own services, which apply their own privacy notices. Following an external link makes a new request to that service.

Requests and updates

Use the operator’s contact channel for privacy questions and state that your request concerns MetroMind. Avoid posting documents, addresses or other personal data in public issues. You can clear browser-local storage directly through your site-data settings.

This notice describes the current version and will be updated when data handling or enabled services change.
