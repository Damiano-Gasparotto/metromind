METROMIND / HOW TO PLAY

LEARN TO READ THE NETWORK

Every transfer is a choice. Every required stop is a constraint. The best route comes from considering the whole mission.

Every mission has three parts

Each game chooses a start, a destination and one or more required stops. Visit all of them and finish at the destination. You choose their order: the list is a set of objectives, not a fixed itinerary.

Reaching the destination before collecting every required stop does not end the game. Passing a required stop during an automatic ride counts as visiting it; no extra button is needed.

Choose a direction and let the train continue

The buttons below the map show the available directions from your station. One choice takes you to the next interchange, branch, terminus, required stop or destination. You do not need to advance one stop at a time.

Read both the line and the direction. On branching lines, the terminus helps you identify where the train is going. You can also tap the endpoint of an available ride on the map.

How game time is calculated

  • Every connection between consecutive metro stops costs 2 minutes.
  • Changing lines adds 4 minutes. Boarding your first line does not count as a transfer.
  • A marked walking connection costs 5 minutes. Boarding after walking does not add another transfer penalty.

For example, 6 metro connections and one transfer cost 6 × 2 + 4 = 16 minutes. A direct ride of 8 connections also costs 16 minutes. Fewer stops do not always mean a better result.

These are puzzle rules. They do not represent service frequencies, waiting times or actual journey times.

A four-step strategy

  1. Locate every required stop. Identify their lines before choosing your first direction.
  2. Group stops sharing a line. One ride may complete several objectives.
  3. Compare interchanges. Avoiding a transfer can compensate for two extra stops.
  4. Try another order. With two required stops, compare both sequences. With three, consider which final visit leaves the easiest route to the finish.

Levels, undo and the solution

Easy has one required stop, Medium two and Hard three. Changing levels starts a new game. Undo reverses the entire previous ride, including its time and collected stops.

Show solution reveals a minimum-time route for the mission. The game considers every visiting order and includes transfer costs. Your final time is compared with this minimum; several routes can be equally valid.

Study before playing

The map atlas lets you search for a station, filter a line, drag and zoom. Each city’s study page includes complete station sequences, useful interchanges and a worked mission. Diagram positions are approximate: count connections when choosing a route.

Phone and offline play

On Android, open MetroMind in your browser and choose Add to Home screen if available. After an initial online visit, the browser caches the game files for offline use. Updates need a connection, and links to external websites require internet access.

Frequently asked questions

Must I visit required stops in the listed order?

No. Choose any order, visit all of them, then finish at the destination.

Why did the train stop at a station without an interchange?

It may be a required stop or your destination. Branches and termini also interrupt an automatic ride.

Can I use the map for an actual journey?

It is a game diagram. It does not include every service in a city or simulate disruptions, timetables and waits. Consult the operator’s official information before travelling.
