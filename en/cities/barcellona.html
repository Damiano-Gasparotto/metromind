How the network took shape

  1. 1924

    The Gran Metro

    On 30 December 1924 the Gran Metro opened four stations between Lesseps and Catalunya. That stretch was the forerunner of L3: in the game you can still follow the route between the two original termini.

  2. 1926

    The Transversal

    In 1926 a different company opened Bordeta–Catalunya, the forerunner of L1. Catalunya brought together two independently developed projects. In the game it is one place where you can switch from Red to Green.

  3. 1951–67

    Towards a municipal network

    After the war, expansion plans resumed and the two networks moved towards municipal ownership. The story was no longer simply one of two companies building separate lines.

  4. 1968–82

    The network expands beyond the centre

    The expansion era took L5 as far as Cornellà. The Cornellà Centre terminus in the game shows how extensions changed the scale of the network.

  5. 1995

    L2 opens

    L2 added a new route to the network. Find Universitat in the game: changing from L1 opens an alternative to the historic Transversal corridor.

  6. 2009

    Building in sections

    The game map shows another result of opening in stages: L9 and L10 have separate northern and southern sections. Look for a route connecting them via other lines rather than assuming that sections with the same number meet.

Sources and archives: Fundació TMB – Cent anys del Metro

How to think about this network

The represented network includes L1, L2, L3, L4, L5, L9, L10 and L11. FGC railway lines and other services are outside the game, so a real-world connection may not exist in this puzzle.

L9 and L10 each have separate northern and southern sections in the model. A matching line number does not guarantee a direct ride: check the sections in the list and trace the connecting interchanges.

For central required stops, look for shared lines first. For outlying stops, consider the cost of travelling out and back on a branch: finishing that detour early can prevent a repeat trip at the end.
